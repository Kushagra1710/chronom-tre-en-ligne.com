Looking polished is less about making clothing appear expensive and more about making each choice appear intentional. Fit, maintenance and proportion are more dependable than visible branding.

## Begin with fit

Check shoulder placement, sleeve length and where trousers break over shoes. A simple alteration can be useful, but it should be weighed against the garment’s fabric and construction.

## Care changes the surface

Follow the care label, remove lint and store garments so they retain their shape. Pressing or steaming suitable fabrics can make the whole outfit read more clearly.

## Reduce visual competition

Use one focal point and let the rest of the outfit support it. Consistent hardware, a controlled palette and clean shoes often create more cohesion than adding more accessories.

## Be honest about limitations

Styling cannot correct every fabric or construction problem. The final guide will explain when it is better to keep, alter, repair or avoid a purchase.

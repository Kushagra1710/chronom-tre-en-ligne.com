Good color combinations usually create clear contrast without making either piece compete for attention. Start with familiar neutrals, then introduce color through one part of the outfit.

## Reliable starting combinations

| Shirt | Trousers | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White | Charcoal | Crisp and high contrast |
| Pale blue | Navy | Tonal and easy to repeat |
| Cream | Olive | Warm and relaxed |
| Burgundy | Stone | Rich color with a quiet base |

## Check undertones in natural light

Two garments can look coordinated indoors and clash in daylight. The final guide will show warm, cool and neutral examples using the same trouser silhouette.

## Use texture when colors are close

A brushed knit against crisp cotton creates separation even when both pieces sit in the same color family. This is especially useful for tonal outfits.

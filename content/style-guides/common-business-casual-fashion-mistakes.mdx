Business casual is difficult because it describes a range rather than one uniform. The safest approach is to observe the workplace and understand which element makes an outfit feel relaxed.

## Treating every office alike

A creative studio, school and finance office may use the same label while expecting different clothing. Context should come before a generic checklist.

## Ignoring fit when an item is technically formal

A blazer or shirt does not automatically look polished. Pulling buttons, restrictive arm movement or excess fabric can distract from otherwise appropriate choices.

## Making every piece casual

Relaxed denim, a graphic tee and athletic shoes may combine into an outfit that falls outside the intended balance. Pair one casual element with clearer structure elsewhere.

## Saving maintenance for special occasions

Wrinkles, worn soles and missing buttons are often more noticeable than price. A short weekly care routine can keep a small work wardrobe dependable.

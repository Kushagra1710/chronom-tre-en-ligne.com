Minimal dressing does not require identical neutral uniforms. It works through clear silhouettes, intentional repetition and details that reward a closer look.

## The twelve working formulas

1. White shirt, straight denim and loafers
2. Tonal knit with wide trousers
3. Plain tee beneath an unstructured blazer
4. Column skirt with a compact cardigan
5. Dark denim with a denim shirt in a different wash
6. Relaxed suit worn with clean sneakers
7. Monochrome base with a textured coat
8. Fine knit tucked into pleated trousers
9. Long dress with a cropped utility jacket
10. Oxford shirt with tailored shorts
11. Minimal sweatshirt with a structured trouser
12. Matching separates with one contrasting accessory

## What the final edit should demonstrate

Each formula will be photographed with repeatable pieces and adjusted for different climates. The final copy should explain fabric weight and proportion instead of presenting minimalism as a shopping list.

The strongest summer ideas are adaptable rather than costume-like. This draft focuses on airy proportions, personal accessories and layers light enough to carry after sunset.

## Planned outfit edit

1. Oversized linen shirt with tailored shorts
2. Ribbed tank with a fluid maxi skirt
3. Knit polo with relaxed chinos
4. Long shorts with a fitted tee
5. Lightweight co-ord with flat sandals
6. Simple slip dress with an open shirt
7. Sports-inspired top with a clean trouser
8. Boxy camp-collar shirt with straight denim
9. Monochrome cotton layers with one bright accent
10. Cropped cardigan over a breathable base
